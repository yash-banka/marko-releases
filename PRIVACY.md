# Privacy Policy — Marko

_Last updated: August 3, 2026_

Marko does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. The app renders
Markdown files locally on your Mac and makes no network connections of its own.
There is no analytics, no tracking, and no account.

## On-device intelligence

Document Intelligence features (summarize, ask, explain) run entirely on your
device using Apple Intelligence. Your document content is never sent to us or to
any server.

## Your files

Files you open are displayed only; Marko never modifies or uploads them. A
document you open may itself reference remote content (for example an image at an
`https://` URL), which your Mac would load the same way a web browser does; Marko
neither adds nor controls that.
